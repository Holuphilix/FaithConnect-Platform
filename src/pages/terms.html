Terms of Use

These initial terms describe the current FaithConnect account service. They require legal review for the countries where the platform will operate before a public launch.

Who may create an account

You must be at least 18 years old and provide an email address you control. Keep your sign-in credentials private and tell us promptly if you suspect someone else has accessed your account.

Current service

At this stage, FaithConnect provides account registration, email verification, sign-in, and password recovery. Profiles, recommendations, interest requests, private messaging, blocking, reporting, and moderation are not available. Creating an account does not guarantee access to future features.

Respectful use

Do not use the account to impersonate another person, interfere with the service, send unlawful content, or attempt to access another member’s account or information. Additional community standards and safety processes must be published before member interactions open.

Account closure

Account deletion and data export controls are not yet available in the interface. Before using this service with real members, FaithConnect must publish a working account closure process and the appropriate contact details.

Changes and availability

The service is under development and may change or be unavailable. We will present updated terms for acceptance if a material change requires it. Nothing on this site represents that matching or messaging is currently offered.

Contact and governing law

Operator contact details and governing-law information have not yet been established. This draft must be completed before the service is offered publicly.
