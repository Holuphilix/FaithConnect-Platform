Privacy Notice

This draft explains the limited account data used by the current development version. It requires privacy and legal review for the countries where FaithConnect will operate.

Information used for an account

When you register, FaithConnect sends your name and email address to its authentication provider, Supabase, and asks it to store your password securely. FaithConnect does not receive or store your plaintext password. The provider handles email verification, sign-in, and recovery.

Sessions and technical requests

After authentication, the server keeps the provider session in HttpOnly cookies. Browser scripts cannot read these cookies. The server processes authentication requests and may receive basic request data, such as an IP address, as required for rate limiting and service operation. Application logs are designed not to include passwords or session tokens.

Faith and matchmaking information

This version does not request or store faith, denomination, location, relationship preferences, profile photographs, interest requests, or messages. These can be sensitive information. FaithConnect will not ask for them until suitable privacy, consent, retention, and access controls are implemented.

Provider and retention

Supabase hosts authentication and its associated account records under the project’s provider configuration. Account retention, deletion, data location, and support contact arrangements must be completed by the operator before launch. Do not use this development service if you need a finalized privacy arrangement.

Your choices

You may choose not to create an account. The current site does not have in-product export or deletion controls. These must be added with a working support contact before public registration begins.

Contact and effective date

The responsible operator, privacy contact, data location, and applicable legal basis have not yet been established. This draft is not a substitute for the completed notice required before launch.
